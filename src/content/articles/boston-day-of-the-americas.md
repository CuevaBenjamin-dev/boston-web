The Boston Bilingual School community celebrates the Day of the Americas as an opportunity to learn about the cultures and traditions of our continent.

## Learning about the Americas

During this activity, students learn about different countries and reflect on the cultural diversity shared across the Americas. Taking part encourages respect, curiosity and dialogue.

## A community open to the world

The celebration supports students as they learn to value different perspectives and live alongside people from diverse backgrounds.
