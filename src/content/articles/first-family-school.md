In August 2026, Boston Bilingual School held its first Family School, a meeting for the families of Primary and Secondary students.

## Talking about support at home

The Psychology Department led a conversation about life at home, family dynamics and the support students need at each stage of their development.

## School and family

The activity offered an opportunity to share ideas for strengthening communication between the school and families and supporting students' learning and well-being together.
