Robotics is part of the learning experiences available to Boston Bilingual School students. Through practical activities, they explore how to design, build and program solutions.

## Learning by doing

Robotics and programming workshops give students opportunities to experiment, propose ideas and review results. These activities help them put logical thinking and creativity into practice.

## Innovation and collaboration

As they work on projects, students share ideas and learn to solve problems together. Technology becomes a tool for learning and innovation within their school education.
